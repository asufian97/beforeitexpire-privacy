BeforeItExpires Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

BeforeItExpires is published by Stillwave Studio. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Contact: sufianwp97dot@gmail.com.

Information you add

Items, dates, notes, reminders, remembered barcodes, and photos or receipts you add are stored on your device in the app's private storage. They are not sent to us. We have no server that holds your items, and no account or sign-in is required.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan a label, receipt, document, or barcode, or take a photo for an item. You can also pick an image from your gallery. Recognition runs on your device using Google ML Kit. Your photos, camera images, and scanned text are not uploaded for recognition.

Product name lookup

When you scan a retail barcode, the app can look up the product name. If Look up product names is on in Settings, which it is by default, the barcode number is sent over the internet to Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, independent open databases, which also receive your IP address as part of the connection. Your items, dates, notes, and photos are not sent, QR codes are never sent, and you can turn this off at any time. Product names come from these databases under the Open Database License.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your device. If you allow it, the app can also use exact alarms so same-day reminders arrive on time.

Google services

Google ML Kit sends Google limited device information, app information, and performance metrics, such as device model, OS version, and processing time, for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Google Play processes Lifetime Premium purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for a temporary pass. Lifetime Premium removes ads. AdMob may collect and share your IP address, device identifiers including the advertising ID, app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Your items, notes, and photos are not shared with advertisers. See Google's data disclosure.

In some regions you are asked for consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

We do not run our own analytics, and we do not sell your data.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports are saved only to the location you choose. Anything you share or upload from there is under your control and that service's terms. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup; it can be moved in a direct phone-to-phone transfer.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings, then Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Because we hold none of your items, we cannot recover them for you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Not advice

The app only tracks dates you enter or confirm. It does not provide medical, legal, food-safety, or warranty-eligibility advice.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
